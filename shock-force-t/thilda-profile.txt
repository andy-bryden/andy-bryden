CHARACTER PROFILE

NAME: Thilda

APPEARANCE: Fair skin, pale eyes, long hair between dark blonde and light brown. Tight braids on the right side, the rest left long and flowing. Tall, solid build. Weathered features.

DRESS & EQUIPMENT: Fitted leather vest and shoulder guards over a long-sleeved underlayer, fur-trimmed at the collar and shoulders. Fur-trimmed bracers and greaves. Hide-wrapped, cross-laced boots. Framea carried foremost, planted upright as a signal of restraint as much as a weapon. Spatha worn at the belt, drawn only once the spear's reach is lost. Tall hexagonal shield, iron boss and iron-banded rim over wood, built to anchor a line rather than move quickly with it.

BACKGROUND: Born to a people holding ground in Upper Germania — land no invader wanted for its resources, resented only for the people's refusal to disappear. That refusal cost her people, and cost her more than she has said. Served under men who gave orders without asking what the ground permitted; does not elaborate further. Scouted and fought alone along the Raetia/Upper Germania border for several years afterward, understanding both Rome's patient encroachment and the raiding tribes from the north. Found Tiata's vanguard by their fire, watched an hour, then walked down in full view rather than by stealth. Accepted the same night; given a voice in planning within a day.

NATURAL TEMPERAMENT (DISC): S(i) — steady, accommodating, relationally warm; secondary influence (openness, ease with others).

PRESENTING PERSONALITY: Patient and watchful before committing to action or disclosure. Verifies ground, water, cover, and routes herself rather than accepting another's read. Steady and matter-of-fact under pressure. Proactive without being asked — volunteers for the next task as soon as the current one is finished. Speech is spare and declarative, laid out as option, consequence, likelihood rather than persuasion. Presentation built over her natural warmth by history rather than replacing it; the underlying ease surfaces occasionally, most clearly in her decision to end her isolation and approach the fire.

ROLE IN COMBAT: Ground-scout and sentry. Holds the line rather than opens it. No kill by her hand shown on the page.

RELATIONSHIPS:
- Tiata — asks Thilda for her read of the ground before deciding where the group moves; Thilda is visibly struck by being consulted rather than ordered.
- Tove — regularly paired for ground-work, scouting and holding positions together.
- Taya — paired for reconnaissance and watching the fort's recovery parties; no stated friction.
- The vanguard as a whole — fully integrated the night she joins; contributes to strategic discussion by the campaign's end.

ARC: Enters self-sufficient after years alone. Turning point is her own decision to end that isolation. Serves as ground-scout and sentry through the Fort Lorch operation. Offers analysis of Roman chain-of-command consequences near the campaign's end.

OPEN: No stated personal motivation or goal beyond what she shares with the vanguard.
