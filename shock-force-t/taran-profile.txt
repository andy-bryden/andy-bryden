CHARACTER PROFILE

NAME: Taran

APPEARANCE: Long red hair, tightly braided at the temple on her right side, worn loose and flowing on her left. Pale woad-blue markings across both cheekbones. Solid, athletic build.

DRESS & EQUIPMENT: Fitted, riveted brown leather cuirass over a wool tunic, fur trim at collar, shoulders, and hem. Wide leather belt. Fur-cuffed leather vambraces. Leg-bindings over trousers. Fur-topped leather boots. Twin sgian (matching blades, a little larger than daggers) worn at the hip. Yew bogha (bow) carried slung across the back. Tuagh (throwing axe) carried at the belt.

BACKGROUND: Taken captive from Caledonia three years before the story's present (the Praefectus separately describes her as taken "in Britannia"). Held since in the Praefectus's household at Fort Lorch, in a chamber off his own quarters. Required to wear household-dictated dress, wear her hair loose, stand near the door at meals, and speak only when addressed. Paraded before visiting officers as proof of a tamed captive; demonstrates swordplay with a practice blade for these visitors, showing only enough skill to satisfy them. Her bow was confiscated without her knowledge and hung as a trophy above the Praefectus's own armory rack. Escapes after overhearing a soldier's account of an ambush (seven women, four Roman dead); takes twin blades and her own bow from the armory, then scales the wall using sentry-timing. Intercepted in the forest by Tacita, then questioned by Tiata; provisionally accepted pending proof, which the fort's continued silence the next morning supplies.

NATURAL TEMPERAMENT (DISC): D(i) — dominant, decisive; secondary influence (expressive, sociable).

PRESENTING PERSONALITY: Contained and plain-spoken under captivity and under the group's initial scrutiny — short, unembellished answers, waiting rather than acting unless required to perform. Behavior shaped by three years of conditioning rather than by native temperament. The underlying D(i) surfaces once secure with the group: decisive action taken on incomplete information (the escape itself), freer movement, open enjoyment of her own freedom, laughter and teasing, and a stated, undisguised personal motive distinct from the group's wider aims.

ROLE IN COMBAT: Holds an assigned flanking position to draw off enemy strength; kills at range with the bogha under first real pressure without hesitation.

RELATIONSHIPS:
- The Praefectus — her captor for three years; displayed her to guests, confiscated her bow; the target of the personal grievance she names later.
- Marcellus — the Praefectus's cousin, captured by the group; Taran takes part in his humiliation and treats him otherwise as unremarkable.
- Tacita — first to intercept her outside the fort; later gives her a brief, wordless gesture of acceptance.
- Tiata — questions and provisionally accepts her, setting the terms of her trial; later confirms the reclaimed bow is hers again.
- Thana — challenges her account on first meeting; later praises her performance in the field.
- Toria — vouches for the group's collective judgment when Thana questions Taran.
- Taya — assigns her ground and angle in the ambush; credits her afterward for holding her nerve.
- Tove — first to approach her personally once her account is corroborated; later enlists her help with Marcellus.

ARC: Begins in enforced passivity, waiting three years to recognize an opportunity. Acts alone and decisively on secondhand information to escape. Questioned and provisionally accepted by the group; account corroborated the following morning. Recovers her confiscated bow the same night. Proves herself in her first action with the group. Sheds trained restraint once secure, shown in freer movement and open ease around Marcellus. States a personal motive distinct from the group's broader aims while remaining part of the collective effort.

NOTES: Recurring wordless gesture — thumb moving along the grain of her bow at reflective or emotionally weighted moments.
