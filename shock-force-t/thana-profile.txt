CHARACTER PROFILE

NAME: Thana

APPEARANCE: Olive skin, dark eyes. Long dark hair, tight braids on the left side, loose and flowing on the right — the shared signature hairstyle worn by all eight women. Lean, athletic build. A faint, near-permanent suggestion of a smirk at rest.

DRESS & EQUIPMENT: Bronze cuirass with lion-head motif at the belt, worn over a short tunic. Layered leather pteruges skirt. Single bronze vambrace on the sword arm. Bronze greaves with lion motifs, worn over leather sandals. Plain pendant at the throat. Xiphos in hand. Twin akontia carried across the back. Xiphidion at the hip as a secondary sidearm. Tall oval thureos shield, wood-faced with a bronze lion boss, carried when the fight calls for it.

BACKGROUND: Greek-born to a family of modest standing in a small coastal town. Taken at fourteen when pirates came ashore at night. Sold into gladiatorial slavery under Servius at the Kynegion in Byzantium. Survived eleven bouts. Escaped Byzantium alongside Toria and Tacita during the staged lion incident, killing pursuers during the breakout.

NATURAL TEMPERAMENT (DISC): C(d) — analytical, exacting, controlled, with an undercurrent of blunt directness.

PRESENTING PERSONALITY: Dry, ironic, needling. Tests people with pointed questions before deciding what she thinks of them. Wears an easy, settled arrogance and a near-permanent smirk. Deflates enthusiasm with flat correction. Taunts a helpless target in a calibrated, domestic register rather than a crude one, and stops once the target stops reacting. Behavior shaped by the arena, where plain calculation read as weakness and had to be dressed in bravado instead.

RELATIONSHIPS:
- Toria — earliest ally; fought beside her against the lion and through the Byzantium escape.
- Tacita — frequent fighting partner in ambushes and in guarding Marcellus.
- Tiata — tested her hard on their first meeting over arrogance and cause; ended in mutual respect once Thana explained the calculation behind her manner.
- Taran — received one of Thana's rare unguarded compliments, after her first kill with the group.
- Marcellus (captive) — primary voice of his sustained taunting.

ARC: Stable rather than transformed. The underlying calculation and the surface bravado are both present from introduction and remain consistent. Sustained close-range taunting of a captive is a new application of an established capacity, not a new trait.

OPEN: No stated personal motivation or goal beyond the group's shared shift from evasion to active damage against Rome.
