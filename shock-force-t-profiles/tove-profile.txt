CHARACTER PROFILE

NAME: Tove

APPEARANCE: Fair hair, tight braids on the right side, long and flowing on the left. Pale eyes, level gaze. Tall, broad build — strength held in reserve rather than displayed.

DRESS & EQUIPMENT: Layered leather over a dark underlayer, riveted and quilted panels reinforcing torso and forearms, wolf-fur collar at the throat. Wide leather belt. Dark leggings. Tall leather boots, creased from wear. Long-hafted, single-handed bearded axe, haft wrapped in worked leather — an early, heavier-headed forerunner to what would later be called a skeggøx. Large round wooden shield, rune-carved face, iron boss. Nothing ornamental.

BACKGROUND: Rugii by birth, from a cold coastal homeland in the far north Rome never conquered. Taken captive by Gothic raiders, traded onward to Hasdingi (Vandal) traders. Won her freedom by letting her captors underestimate her until their carelessness became exploitable. Alone in the forest since, until the vanguard found her dressing a boar and she invited them to sit.

NATURAL TEMPERAMENT (DISC): I(s) — sociable, warm, steady; secondary steadiness (consistency, patience).

PRESENTING PERSONALITY: Matches her natural temperament closely — generous, direct, dryly humorous, unguarded. Answers hard questions about her own past without flinching. In a fight, fast and unshowy, a raw focus at odds with her fireside manner. Physically demonstrative — a hand on the shoulder, an embrace — toward whoever in the group is newest or most unsteady.

ROLE IN COMBAT: Front-line striker. Given tactical lead when trusted with it. Works ground-holding and terrain tasks alongside Thilda.

RELATIONSHIPS:
- Taya — found her in the forest, scouted her before the group approached.
- Tiata — led the group in to speak with her; later gave her tactical lead over the Hasdingi ambush.
- Taran — Tove's most consistent source of physical reassurance.
- Marcellus — extends her usual caretaking instinct to him as a bound captive; same behavior, different context.

ARC: Tested early by the Hasdingi ambush, where the revenge she expected to feel doesn't arrive, and she says so plainly. Settles into the role of the group's steady source of warmth, food, and tactical ground-support.

OPEN: No stated personal motivation or goal beyond having found something she considers worth her time.
