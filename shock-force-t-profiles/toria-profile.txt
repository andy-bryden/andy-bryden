CHARACTER PROFILE

NAME: Toria

APPEARANCE: Olive-brown, sun-deepened skin. Long black hair, tight braids on the right side, long and flowing on the left. Dark eyes, straight brows, flat-set mouth. Compact, athletic build — close-combat musculature through shoulders and forearms.

DRESS & EQUIPMENT: Bronze-and-leather cuirass, midriff bare, over a short leather corselet. Bronze pauldrons, overlapping lames. Bronze bracers, both forearms. Wide leather-and-bronze belt over a bronze-edged leather pteruges skirt. Bronze greaves, leather sandals, no helmet. Falcata carried at the hip, horse's-head pommel. Round shield and spear also carried per reference image.

BACKGROUND: Born in Iberia. Taken as a small child when Rome put down unrest in her province. Raised and trained a gladiatrix in Servius's Kynegion alongside Thana. Co-executed the Byzantium breakout.

NATURAL TEMPERAMENT (DISC): I(d) — outgoing, people-oriented; secondary dominance (forward, assertive drive).

PRESENTING PERSONALITY: Blunt, forward-driving, quick to anger. Acts before a plan is fully formed. Commits fully once committed, without pause to weigh the fight. Behavior shaped by history rather than by native temperament; the underlying I(d) surfaces in flashes — open admiration of others' competence, unguarded wistfulness, empathetic reading of an enemy's psychology — but is not her default presentation.

ROLE IN COMBAT: Direct assault. First through a door, first at a blocked path. No reconnaissance role shown in the text.

RELATIONSHIPS:
- Thana, Tacita — co-founders of the group; escaped Byzantium together.
- Tacita — the one whose look, without words, is enough to check Toria's temper.
- Tiata — tested early with sharpened words; working partnership built through direct joint action.
- Tove — admired openly, checked on personally rather than only tactically.

ARC: Backstory surfaces in stages — involuntary fragments under stress, later a fuller account given voluntarily. Instinct to act shown developing alongside patience, without loss of commitment. Voice shifts over time toward darker, more reflective humor.

OPEN: No stated personal endpoint beyond the shared goal of hurting Rome where it cannot hide the wound.
