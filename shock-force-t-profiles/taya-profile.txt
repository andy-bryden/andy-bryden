CHARACTER PROFILE

NAME: Taya

APPEARANCE: Dark hair, braided with metal ornaments threaded through on the left side, worn loose past the shoulders on the right. Sharp-featured, still and watchful at rest. Lean, functional build.

DRESS & EQUIPMENT: Lamellar cuirass of hardened leather plates, bronze-edged and embossed with medallion motifs at the shoulders, chest, and belt — steppe cavalry construction, flexible at the joints. Baldric worn across the chest supporting the gorytos (combined bow-case and quiver) at the hip, arrows fletched and tipped with trilobate heads. Composite recurve bow carried in hand or unstrung across the back. Long sword worn at the belt as a secondary weapon. Leather greaves and boots, banded to match the cuirass.

BACKGROUND: Born to the Roxolani, one of the Sarmatian peoples. Her father led forty riders serving Rome as auxiliary cavalry for eleven years, paid in grain and land rights. After a Roman patrol raided a village north of their territory, the governor needed someone to blame; her father and his riders were called to a muster, disarmed, and killed. Taya was moving the herd to summer pasture at the time and returned to a burned camp and forty unburied dead. She declined to join surviving family groups still treaty-bound to Rome. She spent three winters alone by choice, tracking and raiding Roman patrols, until she found Thana, Toria, and Tacita doing the same, badly, and joined them.

NATURAL TEMPERAMENT (DISC): C(s) — precise, analytical, reserved; secondary steadiness (patience, consistency).

PRESENTING PERSONALITY: Reserved, observant, exact. Speaks little and elaborates only when pressed. Corrects by realignment rather than contradiction, restating what's true instead of arguing against what's false. Consistently first to notice a threat or relevant detail. Values people by demonstrated worth rather than sentiment, and says so plainly.

ROLE IN COMBAT: Scout and ranged support. Reads terrain and enemy movement ahead of engagement; opens ambushes with the bow from concealment.

RELATIONSHIPS:
- Thana, Toria, Tacita — found tracking a Roman patrol badly; she corrected and joined them.
- Tiata — mutual deference: Tiata defers to Taya's read of terrain and tactics; Taya defers to Tiata's judgment of people and unfamiliar territory.
- Toria — offers direct, practical support in the aftermath of Toria's flashback, naming what she sees without probing further.
- Tove — shares a rare moment of real laughter with her on first meeting.
- Taran — gives her the ambush angle credited for her first kill with the group.

ARC: Temperamentally consistent from introduction onward. What develops is disclosure and role — she begins as an unknown quantity even to her own companions, and states her history plainly only once directly asked. Her function expands from solitary raider to the vanguard's primary scout, entrusted alone with reconnaissance shaping its major operations.

OPEN: No stated personal goal beyond the vanguard's shared aims and continued caution against overestimating their gains.
