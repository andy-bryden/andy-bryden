CHARACTER PROFILE

NAME: Tiata

APPEARANCE: Tall, solidly built. Long hair, dark blonde with a coppery tinge — tight braids on the left side, long and flowing on the right. Weathered, sun-worn features. Pale eyes, flat and steady.

DRESS & EQUIPMENT: Fitted leather cuirass, riveted plates at chest and shoulders, worn over a long dark wool tunic and close-fitted trousers. Heavy fur mantle over the cuirass. High leather boots, worn soft with use. Falx carried two-handed as primary weapon. Sica sheathed at the hip as sidearm. Dress reads as functional, not rank-marked.

BACKGROUND: First appears alone in Dacia, standing over the body of a dead Roman surveyor. No family history, captivity, or prior events stated in the text. Joins Thana, Toria, Tacita, and Taya; described as the first among them to survive by strategy rather than wits alone.

NATURAL TEMPERAMENT (DISC): D(c) — dominant, direct, decisive; secondary conscientiousness (analytically exacting).

PRESENTING PERSONALITY: Matches her natural temperament closely. Speech repeatedly described as flat and direct. Asks pointed questions about motive and does not fill the silence that follows. Repeats a question rather than accept a non-answer. Asks others to decide matters within their own expertise, then hears out each opinion before ruling. Decisions accepted because everyone knows their view was heard.

ROLE IN COMBAT: Close, efficient fighter, typically paired with a partner rather than operating alone. Unhurried and deliberate under pressure; pace in combat matches her pace in command.

RELATIONSHIPS:
- Thana, Toria, Tacita, Taya — original core group; elicits each one's personal history in turn.
- Thilda — asked to choose the group's direction before the route south is decided.
- Taran — states explicit terms of trust upon her capture; later returns her recovered bow.
- Marcellus — orders his armor stripped following his capture.

ARC: Method of questioning and listening before deciding is established at introduction and unchanged throughout. One specific development shown: recognizes, only in Chapter 16, that she has never asked after Taya's own history.

OPEN: Own personal history and background never disclosed in the text.
