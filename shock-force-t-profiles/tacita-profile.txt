CHARACTER PROFILE

NAME: Tacita

APPEARANCE: Olive skin, dark eyes, heavy dark brows. Hair worn in the group's signature style, tight braids at the left temple, the rest long and loose. Solid build, not lean. Watchful at rest; expression gives nothing away.

DRESS & EQUIPMENT: Bronze cuirass over a short maroon tunic. Leather pteruges at the belt. Bronze vambrace on the sword arm. Bronze greaves. Plain leather sandals. Gladius at the hip. Plumbatae carried at the belt. Round shield, dark leather over bronze rim and boss. Compound-issue gladiatrix armor, worn rather than displayed.

BACKGROUND: Sold by her father to settle a debt. Three years a gladiatrix in Servius's Kynegion before the Byzantium breakout, which she planned and executed.

NATURAL TEMPERAMENT (DISC): S(c) — steady, accommodating, deliberate; secondary conscientiousness (precision, restraint).

PRESENTING PERSONALITY: Near-total silence. Speaks only when asked, in as few words as possible. Communicates through gesture — a look, a touch, a blade pointed in a direction — as often as through speech. Behavior shaped by history rather than by native temperament; the underlying S(c) surfaces in flashes (precision under pressure, quiet steadiness, restrained compassion) but is not her default presentation.

ROLE IN COMBAT: Closer, not opener. Finishes engagements already broken open by others. No described strikes or named motions in the text; rendered through outcome only.

RELATIONSHIPS:
- Toria, Thana — co-founders of the group; escaped Byzantium together.
- Tiata — the only person shown drawing a full sentence from her.
- Taran — present at her joining; given wordless comfort by Tacita afterward.
- Marcellus — his captor for the duration of his captivity; releases him at the rescue party's arrival.

ARC: Silence broken once, fully, in a single disclosure; treated by the group as unprecedented. Returns to near-silence after, but shown capable of full sentences and direct answers when required.

OPEN: No stated personal motivation or goal beyond remaining with the group.
