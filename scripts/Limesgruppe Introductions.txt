 
I'd like to introduce a group of fighters, known as the Limesgruppe. Actually, I think I'll let them introduce themselves. It's safer that way. Safer for me, that is.
 
"If you have the courage to address me, call me Thana. It is a Greek name meaning 'death'. Quite apropos. Just mind my xiphos, as it has a nasty habit of getting under people's skin."
 
"I am Toria, Iberian. My name is not Iberian, it is from Latin, but at least the Romans understand who I am - 'Conqueror' - if my falcata leaves them alive long enough to learn my name."
 
"Tacita. Latin. 'Silent' or 'secret'. I am all."
 
"I carry my Sarmatian history in my gorytos and in my name. Tigratavā means 'with the strength of an arrow'. That defines my methods - and the Roxolani."
 
"I am from Dacia and my name is Tiata. It is said to mean 'daughter of the strong', which I know to be true. Another thing I know to be true: a Roman has respect for a falx."

"Hello, my dear! You can call me Tove. An old Norse name meaning 'beautiful thunder'. It seems like an oxymoron, until you see me swinging my beautiful axe in battle. Never mind, we're not fighting at the moment, so you're safe. Yes, I am Norse, Rugii, if it means anything to you."
 
" 'Strength in battle' is the meaning of my Germanic name, Thilda. It defines my role. Nobody can pass my tower shield without first meeting my framea."

"Did that make you jump? And I wasn't even wielding my twin blades! I am a wild Caledonian woman, otherwise known as Taran, which is a Gaelic name meaning 'thunder'. Lightning might have suited me better - I never strike the same place twice - I never need to."

"And I am the Narrator."
