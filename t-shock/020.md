# 20 — Movement in the Dark

They moved twice more before dark, each time a short distance — always toward better cover, never far enough to lose sight of the fort itself.

The fort sat low against the last light, and they made no fire that night.

Taya took the vigil during the hours around first light — the hours most difficult to remain alert, yet most likely to be fruitful.

She set herself further out than the prvious watch, out past the edge of camp where a sleeper turning or a horse shifting it's footing couldn't reach her. She wanted nothing to distract her.

For a long while there was nothing. The fort held its shape against a sky just beginning to turn grey. Not a single torch moved along it. Nothing.

Then something changed — she couldn't have said what.

Not sound, not sight either. Something deeper than either. She froze and narrowed every faculty to focus on the base of the wall.

There it was. A movement. Then nothing. 

Taya held her focus.

Again, slightly north — a flicker at the treeline. Then stillness, complete and absolute.

That was enough.

Taya came back toward the sleepers, and touched Tiata's shoulder once.

"Something came off that wall," Taya said. "One movement, then a second further north, moving into the trees."

"Down," she said. "All of you. Now."

The other five were awake instantly, low against the ground, weapons ready.
