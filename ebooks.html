Operation Caravan (1942)

A high-stakes, long-range desert assault by the British Long Range Desert Group and the SAS on the Italian-held port and airfield of Barce, Libya, resulting in the destruction of dozens of Axis aircraft.

Operation Chariot (1942)

An audacious amphibious raid on the port of Saint-Nazaire in occupied France, where an old British destroyer packed with delayed-action explosives was rammed into the dry dock gates to deny safe harbor to Germany's largest battleships.

Operation Frankton (1942)

A hazardous commando raid in which a small team of Royal Marines paddled folding canoes 60 miles up the Gironde estuary to plant limpet mines on blockade-running German cargo ships in Bordeaux, France.

Operation Postmaster (1942)

A brazen, unauthorized British commando raid into the neutral harbor of Spanish-controlled Fernando Po, where operatives hijacked an Italian liner and German vessels under the cover of a dinner party.

Operation Creek (1943)

A highly unusual covert operation where a volunteer unit of middle-aged British cavalry veterans boarded and destroyed an Axis espionage vessel disguised as a merchant ship in the neutral waters of Portuguese Goa.

Operation Gunnerside (1943)

A legendary Norwegian sabotage mission where a small team of exiled skiers successfully infiltrated the heavily guarded Vemork hydroelectric plant to destroy the heavy water production vital to Germany's atomic ambitions.

Operation Jaywick (1943)

A long-range Allied commando raid launched from Australia, using a disguised former Japanese fishing boat to slip into occupied Singapore Harbour and sink nearly 40,000 tons of enemy merchant shipping.

Operation Thursday (1944)

The massive, second long-range penetration mission into Burma, which utilized a fleet of Allied gliders and transport aircraft to fly thousands of Wingate's "Chindit" troops deep behind Japanese lines.

Operation Tombola (1944-45)

A rogue British SAS attack launched alongside Italian partisans and escaped Soviet prisoners, which successfully overran a German corps headquarters in the Apennines to the tune of a Scottish bagpiper.

Operation Bowler (1945)

A highly restricted Allied precision air strike on the northern Italian port of Venice that successfully devastated German shipping and logistics while strictly avoiding collateral damage to the city's historic architecture.

Raid at Cabanatuan (1945)

A meticulously planned rescue mission by US Army Rangers and Filipino guerrillas who penetrated 30 miles behind enemy lines to liberate over 500 emaciated survivors of the Bataan Death March.

Operation Greenup (1945)

A highly successful American OSS espionage mission that sent a team—including a Jewish refugee and a German deserter—parachuting onto an Austrian glacier to gather intelligence that ultimately saved Innsbruck from destruction.

Operation Rype (1945)

The first and only American airborne operation in Norway, carried out by the OSS Norwegian Special Operational Group to disrupt rail lines and prevent retreating German forces from reinforcing the fatherland.

Operation Semut (1945)

A dangerous Australian Z Special Unit reconnaissance mission that parachuted operatives into the rugged interior of Japanese-occupied Sarawak, Borneo, to organize indigenous Dayak tribes into a lethal guerrilla resistance force.
